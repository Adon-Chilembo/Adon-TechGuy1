About Me

My name is Adon Chilembo, and I am a Cyber Security student at Mulungushi University's School of Engineering and Technology. Alongside my studies, I founded ZedWisdom, a media and content brand that produces Zambian-focused motivational and cultural content. I enjoy combining technical skills with creativity, and I have a strong interest in creative writing, screenwriting, and stage play writing. Building this website is one of my first steps into web development, and I am excited to keep growing these skills so I can create better tools and platforms for the work I do both in class and through ZedWisdom.
